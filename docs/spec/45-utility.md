## Summary
Utility items are the point unlocks from PROG-005: quality of life, mobility, scouting and info, and base defense extras. This file collects their rules, starting with backpacks.

## Requirements

### UTIL-001 — One backpack at a time
**Status:** draft
**Priority:** must

**Description:** Backpacks come from Traveler's Backpack (PACK-004). A player can only carry one backpack at a time. Carrying more makes them too heavy: they get Slowness II and a short message.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A player carrying exactly one backpack (worn or in the inventory) has no penalty.
- [ ] A player carrying two or more backpacks gets Slowness II for as long as they do.
- [ ] While the penalty is active, a short message tells them why (your wording: "2 backpacks worn. Too heavy"), through the lang file.
- [ ] Whether backpacks are point unlocks, and their sizes and upgrades, follow Q-026.
