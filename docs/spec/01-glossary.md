# 01 — Glossary

| Term | Definition | Registry ID (if any) |
|---|---|---|
| Team | A group of players sharing all progression. A solo player is a team of one. | — |
| Tier | An ordered step of progression. Each tier belongs to one planet; a planet has one or more tiers. | — |
| Planet | A world/dimension with its own theme, enemies, resources and difficulty. Earth is the first. | TBD |
| Story milestone | A story event (e.g. a boss fight) that opens the next tier. | — |
| Utility points *(name TBD)* | Currency a team earns and spends on special utility unlocks. See Q-006. | — |
| Locked item | An item the player's team hasn't unlocked: can be carried, but not used or crafted; shown grayed out. | — |
| Player level | Per-player level from combat and quest XP. Level-ups add utility points to the team; no stat bonuses. | — |
| Launch defense | Earth's finale: a horde siege on the base from launch start until rocket liftoff. | — |
| District | A size-limited dimension within a planet, with its own biomes, enemies, tone and unique structures; opened by the story. | TBD |
| Vane group | Faction led by Dexter Vane: rednecks and rich people fighting side by side; allies of the team. Uncut add-on name: Tusk group. | — |
| Mini chicken | Small chicken alien that uses guns picked up on Earth. | TBD |
| Special zombie | A zombie with one clear ability and a counter (e.g. the Rallier). | TBD |
| Rallier | Special zombie that makes nearby zombies faster. | TBD |
| Garage | A building at safe spots that stores vehicles and hands them out through a terminal; usable everywhere once powered. | TBD |
| Hardening material *(name TBD)* | Alien material that hardens blocks so enemies can't break them; not early game, only from the aliens. | TBD |
| Horde night | A night when a big horde attacks the team's base; equally strong each time within a district. | — |
| Enemy tracker | In-game lookup of encountered enemies, with abilities and counters. | TBD |
| Nether (overhauled) | A scary underworld dimension, medium-hard, opened near the rocket; source of the last rocket fuel ingredient. | minecraft:the_nether |
| Unminable block | One of three block types used in protected structures (Reinforced Concrete, Riveted Steel, Alien Alloy); can't be mined, immune to explosions, never obtainable by players. | TBD |
| Faction | An organised group (e.g. the military, raiders) whose attitude toward each team can change. | — |
| Unknown group *(name TBD)* | A mysterious group on Earth, later revealed to be connected to the aliens behind the infection. | — |
| Skill tree | Per-player tree of capped stat upgrades (health, regeneration, movement…), split into sub-trees by category; points come from player levels. | — |
| Specialist sub-tree | One of four special sub-trees in the skill tree; each player unlocks two through quests. | — |
| Downed | The state a player enters instead of dying: crawling, a 10-shot pistol, ignored by enemies, revivable by teammates for 90 seconds or give up to respawn. | — |
| Safe spot *(name TBD)* | A place along the way with a bed already placed (respawn and heal point); some are guarded by friendly redneck defenders. | — |
| Grave | A container left where a player died, holding their items; only that player can open it. | TBD |
| The aliens *(name TBD)* | Ancient, goofy-looking, redneck-themed, part-chicken species (~4,000-year lifespans) that ruined its own planet and spreads a virus to clear new ones. | — |
| Magic planet | The aliens' ruined home world; the only place where magic works. | TBD |
| Magic book *(name TBD)* | The special book used to cast magic on the magic planet. | TBD |
| Dexter Vane | Fictional tech billionaire who was tricked into helping the aliens create the virus. Uncut add-on name: Elon Tusk. | — |
| Uncut add-on | Local-only jar, never uploaded, that swaps in parody names and spicier moments (STORY-019). | — |
| Dodge roll | A quick roll players use to avoid attacks. | — |
| Trinket | An item worn in an extra equipment slot that grants a fighting ability or power. | — |
