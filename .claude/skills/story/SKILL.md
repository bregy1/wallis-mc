# Story

The story is the backbone of Wallis Ultimate. `docs/spec/20-story.md` is the source of truth for plot, characters and lore. This skill says what good story content for this pack looks like. Follow `spec-driven` and `ask-first` as for any other area: mark your own ideas as **(proposal)**, and the user decides.

## The core idea
A group of rough rednecks missed the apocalypse. Earth is overrun by a virus that looks home-grown but was made by ancient, goofy, part-chicken aliens who ruined their own planet and need fresh water. They tricked tech billionaire Dexter Vane (uncut add-on: Elon Tusk) into helping. A cult of human collaborators, led by disguised aliens, does their work on Earth. The rednecks survive, win over factions, uncover the truth bit by bit, build a rocket and reach the Moon, which turns out to be only an alien mini base. Version 1 ends there. Magic and the aliens' real home come later.

## What matters (the user's rules)
1. **The story is important.** It sets the pace of the whole game; it's never an afterthought.
2. **It has to make sense and be somewhat realistic.** Every character acts for a reason. Cause and effect hold. No plot holes. Lore stays consistent (e.g. magic only works on the aliens' home planet). Example of the right kind of realism: the super-rich survived because they had bunkers.
3. **Action first, with lots of rough fun and jokes.** Fights are real-time. Dialogue is rough, funny and full of banter and swearing (there's a clean-language toggle, STORY-002). South Park gives the taste: crude, irreverent satire that spares nobody, with jokes that actually land. Our own jokes in that spirit, never lines lifted from the show. If a line could be in a polite game, make it rougher or make it funnier.
4. **Slightly sexy is fine, now and then.** In the public (CurseForge) pack: flirty jokes, innuendo and suggestive but covered outfits only, no nudity. A few brief moments of mild nudity may exist only in the local "uncut" add-on (STORY-019). Nothing pornographic, nothing too crazy, anywhere.
5. **It has to be exciting.** Twists, betrayals, stakes, set-piece fights, cliffhangers at chapter ends.
6. **Build up slowly.** Hint at every big truth with clues long before revealing it (STORY-016).
7. **Tone changes by planet and district** (STORY-009), but the overall mix is action plus humor.
8. **Building stays part of the story throughout.** The story keeps giving reasons to build: the first home, fortifying against raids, the rocket, and more.
9. **Famous faces, two versions.** The public pack uses fully fictional characters inspired by famous people, with names nobody can tie to a real person. The local "uncut" add-on (STORY-019) swaps in the near-identical parody names (Elon Tusk and friends). Rich and famous people likely survived, so they turn up. Also army characters who love the chaos, and random weirdos.

## Guardrails (Claude's additions; the user can adjust the last three)
- Every character in a sexy moment is clearly an adult.
- Sexy moments never involve parodies of real people, in either version.
- Parodies poke fun at a public persona. Don't base a parody on real-world conspiracy theories about that person (e.g. vaccine or virus conspiracies).
- Rough means swearing, gross-out, dark jokes and roasting everyone, characters and players alike. It doesn't mean real slurs aimed at people for who they are.
- Anything in the public pack must pass CurseForge's rules: no nudity, no real names, project page and description kept clean (PACK-007). The "uncut" add-on is never uploaded anywhere.

## Checklist for any new story content
- Does it make sense? Would this character really do this, and why?
- Is it exciting? What's at stake?
- Is it funny where the tone allows?
- Is the reveal set up by earlier clues?
- Does it fit the tone of its planet and district?
- Does the chapter still give players a reason to build?
- Does it contradict anything in `20-story.md`? If it changes something there, propose the spec change first.
